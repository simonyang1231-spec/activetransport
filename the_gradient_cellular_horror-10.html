THE GRADIENT: CELLULAR HORROR

IGCSE BIOLOGY • ACTIVE TRANSPORT SIMULATION

You are a single Glucose Molecule stranded in the gut lumen. The environment outside the epithelial cell has a LOW concentration of glucose.

The natural force of the Concentration Gradient constantly pulls you downwards into the cellular void. Passive diffusion cannot help you move into the blood stream!

To pass into the cell against the gradient, you must perform Active Transport:

  • Collect ATP Energy Fragments created via cellular respiration.
  • Avoid lethal Protease Enzymes & Parasites patrolling the membrane corridors.
  • Power the Protein Pump Gate at the top of the membrane to escape!
